Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Planning Poker is a free, real-time estimation tool for agile and scrum teams. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What data we collect

We collect the minimum data needed to run a session:

  • Display name — the name you type when joining a room. This is visible to other participants in the same session.
  • Vote values — the card you select during estimation. Visible to all participants after the facilitator reveals cards.
  • Room and session data — room code, story names (if added), estimates, and round results. Stored temporarily in Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Cookie preference — a single value stored in your browser's localStorage to remember that you have accepted this notice. No personal data is stored in this entry.
  • Anonymous usage counts — we record aggregated, anonymous daily counters of feature usage (for example: number of rooms created, stories estimated, and invites copied). These are integer counts only. No user identity, name, IP address, device ID, or session identifier is associated with these counts. They are used solely to understand how the product is being used and to improve it.

We do not collect email addresses, account credentials, IP addresses, device identifiers, or any information that identifies you outside of a session. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. The anonymous usage counters described above do not use cookies and do not identify individual users.

How data is stored

Session data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database, a service provided by Google LLC. Firebase servers are located in the United States. By using Planning Poker you acknowledge that session data is transferred to and processed in the US under Google's standard contractual clauses and privacy framework.

Session data is temporary by nature. Room data is only active while a session is running. There is no persistent user account, login, or long-term storage of personal data.

Fonts

This site uses self-hosted fonts (Outfit and Cormorant Garamond). No font files are loaded from Google Fonts or any third-party server. No data is sent to Google as part of typography rendering.

Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The only browser storage used is:

  • localStorage — to store your cookie consent preference (pp_cookie_ok) and, if you activate a Pro key, a record that your key is valid (pp_pro). These entries contain no personal data beyond the key itself and are never sent to any server except during initial validation against our Firebase key store.
  • Firebase session tokens — Firebase may write functional data to localStorage to maintain your real-time connection. This is required for the product to work and does not contain personally identifiable information.

We collect anonymous, aggregate usage counts (see "What data we collect" above). These are written directly to Firebase and do not use cookies or browser storage. You can reset your cookie consent at any time using the Cookie Settings option in the site footer.

Third-party services

The only third-party service used is Firebase (Google LLC) for real-time data sync. Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

No analytics, advertising networks, social login, or other third-party services are integrated.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

Because Planning Poker does not require an account or email address, we have no way to link session data to a specific individual after a session ends. If you have a concern about data collected during an active session, please contact us immediately.

Data retention

Room data exists only while a session is active. When a facilitator ends a session, all room data is permanently deleted from Firebase. No session data is archived, backed up, or retained after deletion.

Children

Planning Poker is intended for use by professional teams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the product after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at support@planningpoker.app. We will respond within 30 days.
