Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 March 2026

pointpoker is a free, real-time estimation tool for agile and scrum teams. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What data we collect

We collect the minimum data needed to run the product:

  • Display name — the name you type when joining a room. This is visible to other participants in the same session.
  • Account data — if you create an account, we store your email address, display name, plan status, billing status, and account timestamps in Firebase so you can sign in again and restore your account state across devices.
  • Vote values — the card you select during estimation. Visible to all participants after the facilitator reveals cards.
  • Room and session data — room code, story names (if added), estimates, and round results. Stored temporarily in Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Cookie preference — a single value stored in your browser's localStorage to remember that you have accepted this notice. No personal data is stored in this entry.
  • Anonymous usage counts — we record aggregated, anonymous daily counters of feature usage (for example: number of rooms created, stories estimated, and invites copied). These are integer counts only. No user identity, name, IP address, device ID, or session identifier is associated with these counts. They are used solely to understand how the product is being used and to improve it.

We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or behavioural tracking data. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in plain text by pointpoker. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. The anonymous usage counters described above do not use cookies and do not identify individual users.

How data is stored

Session data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database, a service provided by Google LLC. Firebase servers are located in the United States. By using pointpoker you acknowledge that session data is transferred to and processed in the US under Google's standard contractual clauses and privacy framework.

Account sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, also provided by Google LLC. If you create an account, your email address, display name, plan status, and account metadata are stored until you request deletion or the service removes the account. Room data remains temporary by nature.

Room data is only active while a session is running. When a session ends, room data is deleted from Firebase. Account profile data is separate from room data and remains in place to support sign-in, plan access, and account recovery.

Fonts

This site uses self-hosted fonts (Outfit and Cormorant Garamond). No font files are loaded from Google Fonts or any third-party server. No data is sent to Google as part of typography rendering.

Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The only browser storage used is:

  • localStorage — to store your cookie consent preference (pp_cookie_ok) and, if you activate a Pro key, a record that your key is valid (pp_pro). These entries are functional storage only. The Pro key entry may be associated with your signed-in account when you activate Pro.
  • Firebase session tokens — Firebase may write functional data to localStorage or similar browser storage to maintain your real-time connection and signed-in account session. This is required for the product to work.

We collect anonymous, aggregate usage counts (see "What data we collect" above). These are written directly to Firebase and do not use cookies or browser storage. You can reset your cookie consent at any time using the Cookie Settings option in the site footer.

Third-party services

The main third-party service used is Firebase (Google LLC) for real-time data sync and account authentication. Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

No advertising networks or social login providers are integrated at this time. Stripe is referenced in the product for future billing flows but is not yet processing live payments.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Request access to your account data associated with your email address
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

If you use pointpoker without an account, we generally cannot link room data to a specific individual after a session ends. If you create an account, we can identify the account data tied to your email address. If you have a concern about data collected during an active session, please contact us immediately.

Data retention

Room data exists only while a session is active. When a facilitator ends a session, all room data is permanently deleted from Firebase. No session data is archived, backed up, or retained after deletion.

Account profile data remains until you request deletion or the service removes inactive or closed accounts. Password reset emails are sent by Firebase Authentication when requested by the account holder.

Children

pointpoker is intended for use by professional teams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the product after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at support@pointpoker.app. We will respond within 30 days.
