## /planning-poker-jira: why impressions fell 82%

- The drop starts on **17 September**, a week before the 24 September brand suffix was added to its title. Daily impressions went from 8 to 15 (at positions 61 to 80) to 0 to 5.
- Every Jira query sat at positions 60 to 80 (results pages 6 to 8) for five weeks with no clicks. Those deep impressions are what disappeared. The page's average position improved (71.5 to 39.4) and it took its first 2 clicks.
- No other page picked the Jira queries up, so there is no cannibalisation.
- The results page for "planning poker for jira" (checked 1 Oct 2026, UK, non-personalised) is four Atlassian Marketplace apps, a competitor's sync integration page, an Atlassian community thread, an Easy Agile help page and a Reddit thread. The intent is "a plugin that writes estimates into Jira", which Point Poker does not have and the page says so. It ranks for "jira planning poker free" at about 5.
- Conclusion: a reporting artefact of very deep rankings, not a fault. No change made.
